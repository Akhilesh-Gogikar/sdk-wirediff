# Governance

SDK WireDiff currently uses a maintainer-led model. Akhilesh Gogikar is the initial maintainer and release steward.

## Decisions

- Routine changes are decided in pull-request review using tests, synthetic/public evidence, scope, security, privacy, accessibility, and maintenance cost.
- Changes to normalized semantics, command trust, the three-language matrix, governance, or supported runtimes require an issue and explicit maintainer decision.
- Security and coordinated-disclosure fixes may be developed privately and documented after remediation.
- The maintainer may decline sound work that broadens the tool into a gateway, recorder, or provider scorecard.

## Roles

Contributors submit issues, observations, documentation, or code. Repeat contributors may be invited to review demonstrated areas. Commit, release, advisory, and repository-setting access remains least privilege and may be revoked when inactive or unsafe.

## Releases and succession

The release steward verifies [LAUNCH_KIT.md](docs/LAUNCH_KIT.md), approves the changelog, and triggers a source-only release through a reviewed tag. A future successor and effective date will be recorded here before access changes.
