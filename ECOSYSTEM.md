# Optional ecosystem map

The following seven repositories are independent experiments under the same maintainer account. These links are **optional and informational**: no tool is required to install, operate, endorse, or remain compatible with another, and the list is not a bundle or product suite.

- [ReleaseFence](https://github.com/akigogikar/releasefence) — release evidence and policy checks.
- [Semver Weather](https://github.com/akigogikar/semver-weather) — time-filtered dependency reproducibility evidence.
- [ReviewBus](https://github.com/akigogikar/reviewbus) — review-event interoperability experiments.
- [SDK WireDiff](https://github.com/akigogikar/sdk-wirediff) — semantic cross-SDK differential testing (this repository).
- [TokenFlame](https://github.com/akigogikar/tokenflame) — token-usage attribution experiments.
- [MCP Client Autopsy](https://github.com/akigogikar/mcp-client-autopsy) — client behavior conformance evidence.
- [DirectiveGraph](https://github.com/akigogikar/directivegraph) — instruction and policy relationship analysis.

Each repository has its own scope, security model, license, maturity, and release decision. Inclusion here does not claim integration, interoperability, production readiness, or a shared support commitment.
